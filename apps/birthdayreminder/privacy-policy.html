Privacy Policy for Birthday Reminder

Effective date: September 28, 2026

Ant and Buffalo ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Birthday Reminder mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, why it is handled, and the choices available to you.

By using the App, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this Policy, please stop using the App and delete it from your device.

1. Information you enter or provide

The App lets you create birthday reminders. Depending on the features you use, information may include:

  • Names, birthdays or other dates, descriptions, and age-related information that you enter for a reminder;
  • Notification preferences, including notification time, frequency, advance-notification days, theme, and wish-template settings;
  • If you choose Google sign-in or backup: your Google/Firebase account identifier, display name, email address, sign-in provider, and account-related timestamps; and
  • Technical information needed to operate the App and its services, such as app version, device name, and last-opened or backup timestamps.

Please do not enter sensitive information that is not necessary for a birthday reminder. Birthday information about other people is provided by you, so you should have an appropriate basis and permission to store it.

2. How the App uses information

We use information to:

  • create, display, edit, search, and delete your reminders;
  • schedule and deliver local reminder notifications;
  • save your preferences and create optional local backup files;
  • provide optional sign-in, backup, synchronization, and restore across devices;
  • maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the App; and
  • display and measure advertising, as described below.

3. Local storage and optional backup

Reminders and settings are stored on your device for the App's core functionality. The App does not need an account to create local reminders.

If you choose to sign in and use backup or synchronization, the App sends your selected reminder data, preferences, and account profile information to Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore. This cloud copy is associated with your Firebase account and is used only to provide backup, synchronization, and restore features.

The App may also create a backup file on your device when you request a local backup. The App requests Android storage access for this feature. You can refuse that permission; local backup will then be unavailable, while other features may continue to work.

4. Advertising and third-party services

The App uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device or advertising identifiers, IP address, app interactions, approximate location inferred from IP address, and diagnostic information to deliver, limit, personalize, and measure ads, subject to their own policies and your device or Google advertising settings.

The App also uses Google services for authentication and optional cloud storage. These providers process information under their own privacy policies and terms:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Privacy and Security
  • Google advertising technologies

We do not sell your reminder data. We do not use reminder names, dates, or descriptions to serve advertisements.

5. Notifications and permissions

The App uses Android's notification and alarm features to remind you of birthdays, including after a device restart when supported by Android. Notifications are generated for the App's reminder functionality. The App does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, precise location, or calendar for its core features.

6. Sharing and disclosure

We disclose information only as needed to operate the App and its optional services, including to Google Firebase for sign-in and backup and Google Mobile Ads for advertising. We may also disclose information if required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users, the public, or our services.

7. Security

Information transmitted to our service providers is protected in transit using industry-standard transport security. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You are responsible for protecting access to your device and Google account.

8. Retention and deletion

Local data remains on your device until you delete reminders, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App. Local backup files may remain until you delete them separately.

To request deletion of your Birthday Reminder account and associated cloud data, email antandbuffalo@gmail.com with the subject “Birthday Reminder Account Deletion Request”. Include enough information for us to identify the account, but do not send your password. We will verify the request and delete the account and associated Firebase data, except information we must retain for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution reasons. We will delete retained information when the applicable retention requirement ends.

You may also contact us to request correction of inaccurate information or ask questions about data associated with your account.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

10. International processing

Our service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, appropriate safeguards are used for international transfers.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App or applicable requirements change. We will update the effective date above and, where appropriate, provide additional notice in the App or through the store listing.
