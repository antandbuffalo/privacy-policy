Kodai FM – Privacy Policy

Effective 28 September 2026

Kodai FM is an unofficial Android app for listening to the Kodaikanal FM live radio stream. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by All India Radio or Prasar Bharati. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

Information we collect

None. Kodai FM does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party tracking libraries.

Network access

To play the radio, the app connects directly to the broadcaster's public streaming server (air.pc.cdn.bitgravity.com). Like any internet request, that server receives technical information needed to deliver the audio, such as your device's IP address. We do not receive or control this data; it is handled under the stream operator's own policies.

Information stored on your device

The app remembers which radio style you last used (1960s or 1980s). This setting stays on your device, is never sent anywhere, and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Permissions

  • Internet – to stream the radio.
  • Foreground service (media playback) and wake lock – to keep playing when the screen is off or the app is in the background, with controls in the notification and lock screen. Playback only starts when you press play.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.
