Kodai FM – Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026

Kodai FM is an unofficial Android app for listening to the Kodaikanal FM live radio stream. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by All India Radio or Prasar Bharati. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

App analytics

Kodai FM uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand which radio features people use and to improve playback reliability. The app sends Google the following usage and diagnostic data:

  • The radio style you use and when you switch styles.
  • When a listening session starts and stops, its approximate duration in minutes, and whether playback was started from the app, the notification or lock screen, or other controls.
  • The media volume as a percentage when a volume adjustment settles or a listening session stops.
  • A periodic listening-session signal, including while the app is in the background or the screen is off.
  • Stream stalls and playback error codes.

Firebase also automatically collects app activity such as app opens, screen views and sessions, along with information such as the app version, device model and Android version. Google derives approximate location from the network IP address. Firebase Analytics assigns an app-instance ID to distinguish installations and measure usage.

Kodai FM has no user accounts and sends no account ID to Firebase. The app disables collection of the Android Advertising ID and the Android ID (SSAID), and disables personalized advertising in its Analytics configuration.

Analytics data is encrypted in transit to Google. Learn more about how Google handles this data in its Firebase privacy and security documentation and Google Privacy Policy.

Network access

To play the radio, the app connects directly to the broadcaster's public streaming server (air.pc.cdn.bitgravity.com). When it starts, it also downloads a small list of stream addresses from GitHub Pages (antandbuffalo.github.io), so a stream that moves can be fixed without an app update. The streaming provider and GitHub receive the technical information needed to serve these requests, such as your IP address. Each provider handles that information under its own privacy policy.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your radio style preference (1960s or 1980s) and the latest stream address on your device for the next time you open it.

Permissions

  • Internet – to stream the radio, fetch the station list and send analytics data.
  • Foreground service (media playback) and wake lock – to keep playing when the screen is off or the app is in the background, with controls in the notification and lock screen. Playback only starts when you press play.

Children

The app is intended for listeners aged 13 and older.
